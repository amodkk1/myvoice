Setup

The rewriting and web plagiarism search use Claude through the Anthropic API. Your key stays in this browser only (saved locally if you tick the box) and is sent straight to api.anthropic.com, nowhere else.

Remember key on this device

Teach it your writing style

Write about the random topics below in your normal voice. Don't polish it, just write the way you usually do. Aim for at least 300 words in total; around 500 works better. You can also paste something you wrote yourself earlier.

Paste the blog / write-up

Paste the text you want to make original. It gets rewritten in your voice, keeping every fact, number and name, and then checked for overlap with the original.

Avoid AI-sounding phrases (delve, tapestry, "in today's fast-paced world"…)
